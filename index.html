FAQ

Quick answers

Simple details about tips, currency formatting, and equal splits.

How is the tip calculated?

Tip amount equals bill amount × tip percentage ÷ 100. The total bill is the original amount plus that tip.

Does changing currency convert the amount?

No. Currency only changes how numbers are formatted with Intl.NumberFormat. QuickSplit does not perform live exchange-rate conversion.

How does equal splitting work?

The total bill (including tip) is divided evenly by the number of people. Everyone pays the same share.

Where is my history stored?

Recent splits and your theme preference are saved in this browser’s localStorage on your device. Nothing sensitive is uploaded.
