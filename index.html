Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers

Everything you need to know about the math, rounding, and privacy.

How is the tip and split calculated?

The calculation is strictly transparent:

  • Tip Amount = Bill Subtotal × Tip % ÷ 100
  • Total Bill = Bill Subtotal + Tip Amount
  • Amount Per Person = Total Bill ÷ Number of People
How does the "Round up per person" option work?

When enabled, each person's share is rounded up to the nearest whole currency unit (for instance, $17.40 becomes $18.00). The accumulated difference across all people is automatically added to the server's tip as a rounding bonus. No one has to scramble for loose coins!

Does changing currency convert exchange rates?

No. QuickSplit formats numbers according to your selected currency symbol and regional conventions via the browser's native Intl.NumberFormat API. It does not convert exchange rates or require live internet access.

Is my data private? Where is history stored?

100% private. All calculations run entirely inside your browser. Your theme choice, currency preference, and recent splits are saved strictly in your device's localStorage. No cookies, no trackers, and no data is ever transmitted to a server.

Can I use QuickSplit offline?

Yes! Once loaded, QuickSplit uses plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript without any dynamic remote dependencies. It continues to work seamlessly on airplane mode or spotty restaurant Wi-Fi.
